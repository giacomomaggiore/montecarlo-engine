# Portfolio Construction and Risk

A portfolio combines target allocations with contribution, rebalancing, cost, tax, and leverage rules. Risk and return metrics then describe the resulting distribution, not a guarantee about future markets.

## Drawdown

Drawdown measures loss from the prior peak of a unitized return index:

$$
DD_t = \frac{V_t}{\max_{u \le t} V_u} - 1
$$

The index excludes external contributions so deposits cannot appear as market recoveries.

## Terminal wealth and IRR

Terminal wealth is the amount remaining after the model's final liquidation. With an initial investment only, CAGR is informative. When contributions occur, IRR incorporates their date and amount instead.

Loss probability compares each path's terminal wealth with that path's total external contributions. It is distinct from economic ruin, which is reserved for an insolvent leveraged path.
